# Solana Memecoins: Pump or Dump? 🚀📉
### Quantitative Microstructure & Take-Profit Modeling for Pump.fun Solana AMM Launches

This repository implements a production-grade algorithmic pipeline for the Kaggle competition **[Memecoins: Pump or Dump? Predict Pump.fun Launches on Solana](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/memecoins-pump-or-dump)**.

---

## 1. Problem Architecture & The Financial Payoff Metric

At exactly $t = 60\text{s}$ post-migration (from Pump.fun bonding curves to PumpSwap AMMs), the algorithm evaluates whether to **buy** or **skip** the token, allocating an automated take-profit multiple $tp \in [3, 100]$ over a 1-hour holding window:

$$\Pi(tp) = \begin{cases}
0, & \text{if } tp = 0 \quad (\text{Skip / Costless Cash}) \\
tp - 1.30, & \text{if } tp \in [3, 100] \quad \text{and} \quad \widetilde{M} \ge tp \\
\max(\text{final\_x} - 1.30, -1.0), & \text{if } tp \in [3, 100] \quad \text{and} \quad \widetilde{M} < tp
\end{cases}$$

### Key Financial Insights:
- **Execution Cost ($c = 0.30$ SOL)**: Accounts for AMM liquidity pool fees, priority compute gas, and slippage buffers.
- **The Free Skip Option ($\Pi(0) = 0$)**: Across the universe of launches, only $\sim 6.07\%$ reach a sustained $5\times$ target, while failing tokens collapse to $<0.05\times$ entry price (losing $\approx -0.90$ to $-1.0$ SOL).
- **The Hurdle**: Unconditional trading yields an expected loss of $-0.531$ SOL per position. Alpha is generated by **rigorous exclusion**—skipping $>98\%$ of unviable tokens and concentrating capital on the highest-conviction tail.

## 2. Engineered Microstructure Features

1. **High-Frequency AMM Microstructure ($t \in [0, 60\text{s}]$)**:
   - **Terminal Order Flow Acceleration**: Volume in seconds $45-60$ relative to early seconds (`late_sol`, `late_over_early`, `late_buyshare`).
   - **Participant Concentration**: Top-1 wallet volume share (`amm_top1`) and Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (`amm_hhi`).
   - **Predatory Flow Detection**: Fast flippers rate (`flip_rate`), measuring early buyers ($t \le 15\text{s}$) who sold before $60\text{s}$.
   - **Kyle's Lambda ($\lambda$)**: Microstructure price impact per unit volume (`impact_per_sol`).
   - **Whale Accumulation**: Maximum single buy (`buy_max`) and count of large trades $\ge 5$ SOL (`whale_buys`).

2. **Bonding-Curve Phase & Latent Demand**:
   - Reverted instructions (`failed = 1`), capturing unmet buyer demand and network contention (`curve_failed`, `curve_failed_ratio`).
   - Duration to fill bonding curve (`curve_dur`), volume, and unique wallets.
   - Initial AMM opening pop relative to final curve price (`migration_pop`).

3. **Launch & Bundle Mechanics**:
   - Creator wallet balance at deployment (`dev_balance`) and curve filling time (`age_s`).
   - Jito/MEV bundle size (`bundle_size`), bundled buys count, and transaction amounts.
   - Token-2022 program flag (`token2022`), opcode instructions, and gas usage.

4. **Causal Empirical Bayes Participant Reputation**:
   - Wallets and creators active in the first 60 seconds tracked over time.
   - **Strict Causal Embargo**: Outcomes are only credited once the historical token is at least 1 hour old ($mt_j \le mt_i - 3600$). Zero future lookahead leakage.
   - Empirical Bayes shrinkage smoothing small sample sizes toward baseline priors (`w_eb_score`, `w_elite_share`, `w_hit_max`).

## 3. Machine Learning & Decision Engine

- **Two-Stage Cascade Ensemble**:
  - **Stage 1 (Survival Classifier)**: Targets $P(\widetilde{M} \ge 1.50)$ to filter out instant rug pulls and dead liquidity.
  - **Stage 2 (Velocity Forecaster)**: Trains on surviving tokens to predict $P(\widetilde{M} \ge 5.0 \mid \text{survival})$.
  - **Composite Probability**: $P^*(\mathbf{x}) = P_{\text{survive}} \times P_{\text{velocity}}$.
- **Multi-Seed Regularized LightGBM**: 5 random seeds (`[42, 101, 2024, 7, 777]`) with row/feature subsampling.
- **Calibrated Decision Hurdle**: The decision threshold $T^*$ is fixed out-of-fold from recent training days to isolate the top $q^* = 1.0\%$ quantile.
